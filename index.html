About Me

WHO
AM I?

I'm Yunessa Rodriguez, a CTD student at CU Boulder with a desire for creating things that leave an impact. I was born curious and stayed that way! If I see someone do something cool, I'll spend the next few weeks learning it myself.

I draw memorial portraits, experiment with makeup, bake new recipes... and somewhere in between, I've dabbled in music, crafting, baking, and more. I bring creativity into every thing I do.

Making food has always been a huge part of my culture. It's an opportunity to share myself with other people, and add those punches of flavor that mirror my punchy personality. That's really what all my creative work is- sharing a piece of myself with the world.

What Even is Success? +
Since I was young, I struggled heavily with my mental health. Goal setting felt impossible when I couldn't even picture the next day. Living in that headspace forced me to make smaller goals for myself every day rather than trying to piece together the bigger picture. Slowly the picture starts to form itself, much like my art.
Proving What's Possible +
I recognize there are millions of people on the same boat as me. More than anything, I want to prove that we are capable of being more than how our health defines us. Our stories are worth telling, and our work deserves to be seen.
Why CTD? +
I want to create anything and everything in this lifetime that forces people to turn their head for a moment and think. CTD has the perfect framework of a strong focus on both creation and impact. I can't picture only doing one thing during this lifetime, and I don't have to.
